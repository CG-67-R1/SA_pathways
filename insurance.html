Without Motorcycling Australia, for now

A new insurer is possible for coaching. It is not a swap-in for race cover.

This page is a warning label, not a shopping list. Nobody should ride until a licensed broker has written a certificate. This project will not help anyone run unsanctioned racing.

How the usual cover works

Motorcycling Australia runs a national insurance program through its own insurance company. Clubs get onto it by affiliating with the state body (Motorcycling SA). Cover applies only while there is an approved permit. There is no “Motorcycling SA but not Motorcycling Australia” version. Leaving one leaves both.

Do not treat a bike-damage sticker as permission to race.

What this means for the project

  • You cannot run Motorcycling Australia club racing or the Oceania Junior Cup on the loop while they are out of the picture. That is the “MotoGP pathway” photograph. Living without it is a cost, not just paperwork.
  • The owner, Playford and any grant will still want a certificate that names them. A broker can try. It will be slower and dearer than the national program.
  • Come-and-try, coaching and closed hire are the activities a broker is most likely to quote. Competitive racing is the activity they are least likely to quote without a controlling body.
  • If a broker will not write it, that is the answer. The venue stays closed.

When this work happens

After the trust exists and the lease shape is on paper. Sit with a motorsport-experienced broker. Ask for public liability that can name the owner and Playford. Take the written exclusions back to the project lead before anyone is invited. Keep Motorcycling Australia as a later option, not a burned bridge.

Do not lead with insurance in a political meeting. Land, Playford and the MotoGP fund still come first. If a minister asks who insures it, the honest line is: a licensed broker, certificate before any riding, and we will not open without it.
