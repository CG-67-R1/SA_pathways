People who hold a lever

Helpful does not mean “email them this afternoon”.

These are the people who can move land or money. The working group already has a lead — Brian Rosser — and a single trail to the Sport Minister’s office. Contacts are listed so the group understands the map. Do not use them unless the project lead has said so.

Do not contact the landowner, Motorcycling SA, council or any minister from this page. Uncoordinated emails look like a complaint, not a project.

1

Nick Champion MP

Local member for Taylor, and Minister for Planning and Housing. He can protect the raceway’s existing use, or let housing close it. He has already approved nearby lots that needed a noise barrier because of this circuit.

Helpful if the ask is small, quiet and already-cleared. Hostile if the ask is “reopen the wall” or “stop your houses”.

2

Rhiannon Pearce MP

Minister for Recreation, Sport and Racing, and also Local Government. She writes sport cheques and talks to councils. She has already cut ribbons on Playford projects (Argana, McGilp). Member for King, on Playford’s eastern edge. A parliamentary office has already written to her on this project. The state sport agency has been in touch. Do not start a second trail.

2a

Lawrence Ben MP

Member for Enfield. Assistant Minister. He is the office that wrote to Pearce. He is not the member for Virginia and he cannot rezone the land. Stay on that existing thread. Do not email him from this page.

3

Premier Peter Malinauskas

Owns MotoGP. Only he can create an $8 million leftover fund the way Gather Round created football’s fund. Do not ask him for asphalt. Ask him to attach a pathway fund to the event.

4

Emily Bourke MLC

Minister for Tourism (from March 2026). MotoGP sits with tourism. The word “legacy” in the event story sits with her department. She cannot rezone Virginia. Zoe Bettison is no longer tourism minister.

5

Matt Burnell MP

Federal member for Spence, which covers all of Playford. His name goes on a Thriving Suburbs support letter. He cannot create the state fund.

6

Mayor Glenn Docherty

City of Playford. Council cash and the sports strategy. Without Playford, the federal application looks orphaned. His price is community access, youth, replaced parking, and the curfew kept.

When — and only when — meetings are authorised

  1. Stay on the existing Ben → Pearce thread. Do not open a second Sport letter.
  2. Champion (land)
  3. Playford Mayor and CEO (applicant)
  4. Pearce (sport program and council permission) — through the trail already open
  5. Tourism / SATC (legacy sentence)
  6. Burnell (federal letter)
  7. Premier, only after the others have not said no

Who is not in the first conversation

  • Motorcycling Australia — out of scope for now. Football had the AFL in the photo. Living without that photo is a political cost, not a reason to stop.
  • Motorcycling SA — not the lead. Not the applicant. Not the trustee.
  • The landowner — a lease counterpart, not a political champion. One conversation, when authorised.
  • Federal Sport minister — useful later for language. Not the capital door.

Ministers move after reshuffles. Names on this page were checked against Parliament of South Australia on 4 October 2026.
