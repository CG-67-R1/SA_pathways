Two-minute read

Keep a place in South Australia where juniors can learn to race on a sealed track.

Adelaide is about to host MotoGP. The only two working sealed circuits are owned by one company. The last public-access circuit in the north — Adelaide International Raceway at Virginia — is sitting idle while houses walk toward the fence.

Please do not contact anyone yet. Do not ring or email the landowner, council, ministers or Motorcycling SA unless the project lead has asked you to. This site is for sharing the facts, not for starting a campaign on your own.

The first build is a small, quiet junior loop on the outside car park — not a rebuild of the old Super Bowl wall.

The situation, in four facts

The eventMotoGP in Adelaide, 2027–2032. About $96 million on the street circuit. No published junior pathway attached.
The monopolyThe Bend and Mallala are both under Peregrine. Club access has become too expensive for a volunteer sport.
The landAdelaide International Raceway, Virginia. Built 1972. Last circuit racing 1999. City of Playford. Privately owned.
The openingFootball got an $8 million facilities fund as a leftover from Gather Round. Motorcycle racing has not been offered the same deal.

What this project is

A community not-for-profit would take a long lease on the raceway and build an 800 metre to 1.2 kilometre junior-safe short circuit on the existing outside paddock. The old bowl and walls stay put. Parking that the loop uses has to be replaced. The venue already has an 11 pm curfew.

It is independent of Motorcycling SA. Motorcycling Australia is not a partner at this stage. Insurance for coaching and come-and-try would be arranged with a commercial broker later.

Words used on this site

AIR
Adelaide International Raceway, Port Wakefield Road, Virginia.
Playford
City of Playford — the local council.
ORSR
Office for Recreation, Sport and Racing — the state sport agency.
SATC
South Australian Tourism Commission — they buy major events such as MotoGP.
Thriving Suburbs
The federal grant that can pay $250,000 to $15 million in Greater Adelaide. This is the right federal door.
CRSFP
Community Recreation and Sport Facilities Program. State sport grant. Cap $750,000. Cannot build a whole circuit.
NFP / trust
A not-for-profit community company. Government will not give circuit money to a private owner.
