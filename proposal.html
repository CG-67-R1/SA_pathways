What we are asking for

Four requests. Not a fantasy national circuit.

The public ask is a community lease, a junior-safe academy on the outside car park, and a MotoGP leftover that looks like the deal football already got.

Please do not send this as a letter yet. The trust does not exist, the lease is not signed, and nobody has been authorised to contact ministers.

1

Protect the land

Keep Adelaide International Raceway as community motorsport and recreation on the Virginia housing front. If the use is not locked, the land becomes the next subdivision.

2

Create an $8 million pathway fund

A three-year Motorcycle Pathway and Facilities Fund as a formal leftover of Adelaide MotoGP 2027–2032. Football’s Gather Round fund was $8 million, then another $8 million. Same shape. Different sport.

3

Federal Thriving Suburbs

City of Playford and a community trust apply for $250,000–$15 million for the junior loop and community facilities. Virginia is in Greater Adelaide, so this is the right federal door — not Growing Regions.

4

Rules on any public dollar

Published junior and community days. Keep the 11 pm curfew. Grant-funded works must survive a sale. No public money as a free upgrade for a private landowner.

Who would run it

An independent South Australian not-for-profit — a clean community trust — with road-racing clubs at the table and Playford as the grant partner. Motorcycling SA is not the lead. Motorcycling Australia is not a partner in this version.

What we are not asking for

  • We are not asking government to buy the raceway or to attack The Bend as a business.
  • We are not asking to reopen the 1972 Super Bowl wall for superbikes.
  • We are not asking for “the $30 million The Bend got”. That figure is a myth. Government paid for services and access, not the whole park. Details are on the money page.
  • We are not asking SATC (tourism) to pour the asphalt. They can only attach the word “legacy” to MotoGP.
  • We are not pitching a sealed circuit at Acusa Park. That idea already failed a feasibility study.

The sentence for every conversation

Adelaide International Raceway is the only sealed circuit left in Greater Adelaide that is not owned with The Bend and Mallala. It is 26 minutes from the city that is about to host MotoGP. Housing is walking toward it. The ask is a community lease, a curfewed junior loop on the outside car park, and a MotoGP pathway fund — not another private showpiece.
