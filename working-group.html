Project Apex — the history that matters

In March 2026 a volunteer team (Project Apex) completed a sealed-circuit feasibility study at ACUSA Park, Motorcycling SA’s off-road venue at Harrogate in the Adelaide Hills. The clubs listed below were already in that room.

The study found:

  • A sealed bitumen loop of about 1.2 kilometres is technically possible on that land, and fire and environment advice was manageable if riding stayed on cleared asphalt.
  • A 1.5 kilometre or longer Motorcycling Australia championship circuit, with proper runoff, does not fit.
  • A 1.2 kilometre track is not attractive enough to the road-race clubs to carry a competition calendar or the money model.
  • Overall feasibility at ACUSA Park is low. The written recommendation is: do not proceed there. Keep the paperwork as a toolkit for another parcel.

That other parcel is now specified. Apex itself named Adelaide International Raceway as the last public-access sealed circuit in the north, sitting idle while houses walk toward the fence. The working group’s first build is a junior-safe loop on AIR’s south-western car park — not a second Hills hunt, and not a 1.5 kilometre championship circuit on a car park.

What this gives a minister or a council. The group has already done the unglamorous work other sports had to show: a named lead, named clubs, a site that was tested and parked, a site that is live, and a product that fits the land. It is not a Facebook idea.

These clubs were part of the Apex process and are the same names now pointed at Virginia:

  • Phoenix Motorcycle Club of SA

  • Historic Motor Cycle Racing Register of SA
  • Atujara Motorcycle Club
  • Ducati Owners Club of SA (DOCSA)

A car-club proposal (Sporting Car Club of SA) is already looking at the existing 1972 circuit. That is complementary, not a fight: bikes take the south-western car park; cars can stay on the old asphalt. The Super Bowl wall is nobody’s first product.

What the group has already put on paper

The pad

South-western spectator car park, about 770 metres by 270 metres. More than 1,000 car parks plus the pits stay. That is the answer to “you removed the car park”.

The club-cost problem

State titles at The Bend, late August 2026: 42 entries, 32 solos. Club hire about $35,000 plus about $4,500 a day for medical. A meeting that size loses about $20,000. That is a spreadsheet, not a feeling.

The political trail

A parliamentary office has already written to the Minister for Recreation, Sport and Racing. The state sport agency has been in touch. Do not start a second letter.

The vehicle

A new local community body, with the clubs in the room. Motorcycling SA is not the lead. Motorcycling Australia is not a partner in this version.

What still has to be built

History is not a grant. The group still needs the legal trust, a long community lease, Playford in the photo, and a calm 8–12 page plan. Those steps are on the homework page.
