The homework list

Other sports had to put paper on the table. So do we.

Football’s $8 million fund was written into an event deal after SANFL published a 10-year plan. Soccer spent 2017 writing a business case. Playford clubs only got grants after they were named in a council strategy. There is no shortcut past this stack. The working group has already finished some of it.

Already on the table (see the working group): a named lead (Brian Rosser); named clubs from Project Apex; a completed Hills feasibility that parks ACUSA Park; a measured AIR pad (about 770 m × 270 m) with 1,000+ parks left; club hire numbers from the 2026 state titles; and a single trail to the Sport Minister’s office. Still missing: the legal trust, the long lease, Playford, and the 8–12 page plan.

Do this in order

Can be prepared now (no one needs to be contacted)

  1. Incorporate the community trust. Get an ABN. Write the constitution so it can hold a lease and grant assets.
  2. Pull the state planning map and the title for the Virginia land. Keep a screenshot. Do not guess the zone name.
  3. Walk the south-western car park against the 770 × 270 m figure: sealed or dirt, nearest house, confirm the 1,000+ remaining parks. Take photos. A concept overlay already exists — it is not a survey.
  4. Write the short pathway plan (problem, product, users, stages, noise, who runs it).
  5. Write the two-page MotoGP leftover paper in tourism language.
  6. Draft a business case with cost bands, operations, and the lease terms.

Only when the project lead says so

  1. Ask the owner for a long community lease — not a weekend hire. Do not talk about grants until the term is in writing.
  2. Ask Playford to name the junior academy in the next sports strategy, and to be the federal grant partner.
  3. Then the local member (land), the sport minister (program), tourism (the word “legacy”), and the federal MP (a support letter).

Only after the trust and the lease shape exist

  1. Thriving Suburbs application, Playford plus the trust.
  2. The next state facility grant, if the trust is 12 months old, for amenities.
  3. A licensed broker for insurance that names the owner and the council, before anyone rides.

Until the trust and the lease exist, the honest meeting is about land and strategy — not “please fund $4 million of asphalt”. Football would not have walked in empty-handed. Neither should we.

How long those other projects took

  • Soccer: business case 2017, open 2022.
  • Netball: planning 2021, big announcement 2024, outdoor courts 2026.
  • Football fund: plan 2022, leftover written into the 2023 event deal, cheques from 2024.
  • Dwight Reserve: named in the 2023 strategy, then a grant.
